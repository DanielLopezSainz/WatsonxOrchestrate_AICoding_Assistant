CITY OF UTOPIA
BUILDING PERMIT GUIDE

This guide tells residents when a building permit is required, how to apply,
what the fees are, and how long a decision takes.


WHEN A PERMIT IS REQUIRED

A building permit is required before you start any new building, extension,
or structure on your property.

Exception: detached garden structures do not need a permit if both of the
following conditions are met:
  - The floor area is under 10 square metres.
  - The height is under 2.5 metres.

If your garden structure meets both conditions, no permit is needed.
If your garden structure exceeds either limit, a permit is required even if
it is detached and in the rear garden.

Examples:
  - A garden shed of 8 square metres and 2.2 metres high: no permit needed.
  - A garden shed of 12 square metres: permit required.
  - A garden structure of 9 square metres but 3 metres high: permit required.
  - A home extension of any size: permit required.
  - A new garage: permit required.


HOW TO APPLY

Applications are submitted online at:
  https://services.utopia.example/permits

You must include with your application:
  - A site plan showing the location of the proposed structure on your plot.
  - Drawings showing the dimensions and appearance of the structure.

Incomplete applications will not be processed. The Permits and Planning
department can advise you on what drawings are needed:
  Phone: 555 0110
  Email: permits@utopia.example
  Hours: Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00


FEES

The fee depends on the floor area of the structure you are applying for:
  - Floor area under 50 square metres: fee is 120.
  - Floor area of 50 square metres or above: fee is 300.

The fee is paid at the time of application. It is not refunded if the
application is refused.


PROCESSING TIME AND DECISION

A decision is given within 30 days of a complete application being received.

You must not start work before you receive the decision. Starting work
without a permit, or before the decision is issued, is a breach of planning
law.

If you have not received a decision after 30 days, contact the Permits and
Planning department:
  Phone: 555 0110
  Email: permits@utopia.example
  Hours: Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00
