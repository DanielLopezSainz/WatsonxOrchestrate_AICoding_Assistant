CITY OF UTOPIA
WASTE SORTING RULES

This guide tells residents which bin to use, how to dispose of glass, what
to do with bulky items, and where to take hazardous waste.


THE FOUR BINS

Green bin: food waste and garden waste.
Blue bin: paper and cardboard.
Yellow bin: plastic and metal packaging.
Grey bin: everything else that does not belong in the other bins.

When in doubt, use the grey bin.


GLASS

Glass bottles and jars go to the street glass containers, not to any of the
four bins.

Important exceptions: the following items are not accepted as glass and must
not be put in the street glass containers:
  - Broken mirrors
  - Window glass
  - Drinking glasses

Wrap these items carefully and put them in the grey bin.


BULKY ITEMS

Bulky items such as furniture and large appliances are collected on request.
They are not placed in any of the four bins.

To book a collection:
  - Book online at: https://services.utopia.example/bulky
  - Up to three items per booking.
  - Collection takes place within ten working days of your booking.

Contact Waste and Recycling for help:
  Phone: 555 0130
  Email: waste@utopia.example
  Hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00


HAZARDOUS WASTE

Paint, batteries, and chemicals must not be put in any of the four bins or
at the street glass containers.

Take them to the recycling centre:
  Address: 14 Mill Road
  Opening hours: Saturdays, 08:00 to 13:00

Contact Waste and Recycling for further information:
  Phone: 555 0130
  Email: waste@utopia.example
  Hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00
