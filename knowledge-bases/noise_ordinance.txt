CITY OF UTOPIA
NOISE ORDINANCE

This guide tells residents what the quiet hours are, when construction work
is allowed, how to get an exemption for a private event, and how to report
a noise complaint.


QUIET HOURS

Quiet hours are the times when noise must be kept to a minimum.

Sunday to Thursday: quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00.
Friday and Saturday: quiet hours are from 23:00 to 08:00.

During quiet hours, music must not be audible outside the property.


CONSTRUCTION WORK

Construction work is subject to separate time limits regardless of quiet
hours.

Weekdays (Monday to Friday): construction is allowed from 07:00 to 19:00.
Saturdays: construction is allowed from 08:00 to 13:00.
Sundays: construction work is not allowed at any time.

These limits apply to all construction, renovation, and power tools used
outdoors or where noise can be heard from neighbouring properties.


EXEMPTION FOR A PRIVATE EVENT

A resident who is planning a private event that will generate noise during
quiet hours can apply for a one-night exemption.

How to apply:
  - Submit the request online at: https://services.utopia.example/noise
  - The request must be submitted at least five days before the event.

An exemption covers one night only. A new request is needed for each event.


HOW TO REPORT A NOISE COMPLAINT

Noise complaints are reported online at:
  https://services.utopia.example/noise

Use the same address whether your complaint is about a neighbour, a
construction site, or a business.
